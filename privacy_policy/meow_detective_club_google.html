Meow Detective Club Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy applies to Meow Detective Club (the “Game”), provided by ChangzaiJoy (“we,” “us,” or “our”), with Android package name com.meowdetectiveclub.game. It explains how information is handled and the choices available to you.

1. Game features and local data

The Game is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require registration or login and does not provide developer-operated cloud saves, social features, or online leaderboards. Level progress, stars, best times, Fish Coins, check-in records, detective ranks, language preferences, and other settings are stored locally on your device to provide game features. We do not upload these game saves to our own servers.

The Game does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise location. Core single-player gameplay does not depend on advertising network requests. Loading and displaying ads requires an internet connection.

2. AdMob advertising and information it processes

The Game integrates only Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) as its third-party advertising service. It does not integrate other advertising platforms, standalone analytics SDKs, or social login SDKs. AdMob itself includes advertising measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

Depending on the SDK version, device settings, applicable region, and your privacy choices, AdMob collects and shares the following types of information:

  • IP address: which may be used to estimate a device’s general location.
  • App and ad interactions: such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics: such as app and SDK performance, startup time, hangs, and energy usage.
  • Device and account identifiers: such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device.

This information supports ad delivery, ad measurement, advertising service analytics, and fraud and abuse prevention. Ad personalization depends on applicable rules, advertising settings, and your choices. Non-personalized ads may also process some data for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google and partners involved in ad delivery may store, access, and process information through SDKs, device identifiers, or similar technologies. See Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and AdMob privacy information.

3. Use and disclosure

We use local game data to save progress and operate the Game. AdMob and its advertising partners process advertising data for the purposes described above. Single-player operation does not mean third-party advertising collects no information. We do not sell your personal information or provide local game saves to advertising platforms.

If you contact us by email, we process the email address, message, and attachments you voluntarily provide only to handle inquiries, bug reports, privacy requests, and necessary related records. Please avoid sending unrelated sensitive information. We do not disclose your contact information to third parties except for service processing described in this policy or when legally required.

4. Retention, deletion, and security

Local game data normally remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game. You can clear data from the app’s storage page in Android settings; this deletes local progress and settings. Any applicable operating system backup or restoration is managed by your device and system settings. We do not operate that backup service.

Contact emails are retained only as needed to resolve requests, provide necessary follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations, and are then deleted or anonymized. You may request deletion using the email below. If information must be retained, we will explain why.

Google retains and deletes advertising data under its policies and applicable requirements. Clearing local data or uninstalling the Game does not automatically delete advertising data already processed by Google. You may submit requests using Google’s privacy tools or the methods listed in its policies.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to our processing. According to Google’s SDK disclosures, user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

You can use available Android advertising privacy settings to manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID; options vary by system version. You can also manage applicable Google ad preferences in Google My Ad Center. These actions may not stop all advertising or all advertising data processing.

Where consent is required, relevant advertising data processing should take place only after the necessary consent is obtained. Where the Game provides an advertising consent message or privacy options entry point under applicable requirements, you can use it to manage or withdraw relevant choices. Withdrawal does not affect processing based on consent before withdrawal.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, obtain data portability, and complain to a local data protection authority. Contact us using the email below. For information independently processed by Google, you can also exercise rights through Google’s privacy tools.

6. Intended age and minors’ privacy

The Game is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18. The Game does not provide account registration, chat, or personal profile forms and does not collect minors’ names or contact details through such features. Advertising SDK processing is described in Section 2.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided personal information to us while using the Game, please contact us at the email below. We will investigate and take applicable deletion or other protective measures for relevant information we hold. For information independently processed by Google, we will explain the relevant privacy request channels.

7. International processing and external links

Google and its partners may process advertising data on servers outside your country or region. Please consult Google’s Privacy Policy for applicable international transfer safeguards. Ads and links in this policy may lead to third-party websites or services, whose own privacy policies govern their information practices.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when game features, advertising services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. For material changes, we will provide notice and obtain renewed consent where required.

喵喵侦探社 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年10月20日

本隐私政策适用于 ChangzaiJoy（以下简称“我们”）提供的《喵喵侦探社》（Meow Detective Club，以下简称“本游戏”），Android 包名为 com.meowdetectiveclub.game。本政策说明本游戏如何处理信息，以及您可以如何管理这些信息。

1. 游戏功能与本地数据

本游戏为单机益智游戏，无需注册或登录账号，不提供开发者运营的云存档、社交或在线排行榜服务。关卡进度、星级、最快纪录、小鱼干币、签到记录、侦探等级、语言和其他游戏设置保存在设备本地，用于提供游戏功能。我们不将这些游戏存档上传至自有服务器。

本游戏不要求您提供姓名、电话号码、通讯录、照片、麦克风录音或精确位置信息。核心单机玩法不依赖广告联网请求；广告加载与展示需要网络连接。

2. AdMob 广告及其处理的信息

本游戏仅集成 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）作为第三方广告服务，未接入其他广告平台、独立统计分析或社交登录 SDK。AdMob 本身包含广告效果衡量、诊断和反作弊处理。

根据 SDK 版本、设备设置、适用地区及您的隐私选择，AdMob 会收集和分享下列类型的信息：

  • IP 地址：可用于推断设备的大致位置。
  • 应用与广告互动：例如应用启动、点击和视频观看情况。
  • 诊断信息：例如应用及 SDK 的性能、启动时间、卡顿和能耗情况。
  • 设备及账号相关标识符：例如 Android 广告 ID、应用集 ID，以及适用时与设备上已登录账号相关的其他标识符。

这些信息用于投放广告、衡量广告效果、分析广告服务表现，以及防范欺诈和滥用。广告是否个性化取决于适用规则、广告配置和您的选择。非个性化广告也可能为投放、效果衡量和防欺诈处理部分数据。

Google 及参与广告投放的合作伙伴可能通过 SDK、设备标识符或类似技术存储、读取和处理相关信息，具体处理方式请参阅：Google 隐私政策、Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息和AdMob 隐私信息。

3. 信息使用与分享

我们使用本地游戏数据保存进度并运行游戏。广告相关数据由 AdMob 及其广告合作伙伴按上述目的处理；单机游戏不代表第三方广告不收集信息。我们不出售您的个人信息，也不将本地游戏存档提供给广告平台。

如果您通过邮件联系我们，我们会处理您主动提供的邮箱地址、邮件内容及附件，仅用于处理咨询、故障反馈、隐私请求及相关必要记录。请勿发送与问题无关的敏感信息。除本政策所述的服务处理或依法必须披露的情况外，我们不会向第三方披露您的联系信息。

4. 保存、删除与安全

本地游戏数据通常在设备中保存至您清除应用数据或卸载游戏。您可以在 Android 系统设置的本应用存储页面清除数据；此操作会删除本地进度及设置。系统备份或恢复功能如适用，由您的设备与系统设置管理，我们不运营该备份服务。

联系邮件仅在处理请求、必要后续沟通或履行适用法律义务所需的期间内保存，之后删除或匿名化。您可通过下方邮箱提出删除请求；如有必须保留的信息，我们会说明原因。

Google 处理的广告数据依照其政策及适用要求保留和删除。清除本地数据或卸载游戏不会自动删除 Google 已处理的广告数据。您可使用 Google 的隐私工具或其政策列明的方式提交请求。

我们采取与数据处理相适应的合理安全措施。根据 Google 的 SDK 数据披露说明，Google Mobile Ads SDK 收集的用户数据使用 TLS 加密传输。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

5. 您的隐私选择与权利

您可以通过 Android 设置中提供的广告隐私选项管理、重置或删除广告 ID，具体选项因系统版本而异，也可通过 Google 我的广告中心管理适用的 Google 广告偏好。这些操作不一定停止所有广告或所有广告数据处理。

在需要取得同意的地区，相关广告数据处理应在取得必要同意后进行。如游戏依据适用要求提供广告同意提示或“隐私选项”入口，您可通过该入口管理或撤回相应选择；撤回不影响撤回前基于同意进行的处理。

根据您所在地的法律，您可能享有访问、更正、删除个人信息，限制或反对处理，数据可携带，以及向当地数据保护机构投诉的权利。请通过下方邮箱联系我们；涉及 Google 独立处理的信息，也可直接通过 Google 的隐私工具行使权利。

本游戏面向18岁及以上的成年人，不以未满18岁的未成年人为目标受众。本游戏不提供账号注册、聊天或个人资料填写功能，也不会通过这些功能收集未成年人的姓名或联系方式。广告 SDK 的数据处理见第2节。

如果您是家长或监护人，认为未成年人在使用本游戏时向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。我们会核实情况，并对我们持有的相关信息采取适用的删除或其他保护措施；涉及 Google 独立处理的信息，我们会提供相应的隐私请求渠道说明。
