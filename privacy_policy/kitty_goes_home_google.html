Kitty Goes Home is provided by ChangzaiJoy. Contact changzai.joy@gmail.com with questions about this policy or data handling.

Local game data.

Progress, stars, daily records, collections, language and audio preferences are stored on your device. The game has no separate game account or cloud progress synchronization. Reinstallation, clearing app data or changing devices may remove these records.

Advertising.

The game uses Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform. Depending on configuration, region and applicable consent choices, these services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad impressions and interactions, performance and diagnostic information. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's disclosure guidance. Applicable ad privacy options are available in the app.

Processing supports ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. IP addresses may indicate approximate location. Non-personalized ads may still process data for these purposes. Where applicable, use the in-app advertising privacy options to manage or withdraw choices. Processing requiring consent should begin only after necessary consent is obtained; withdrawal does not affect prior processing based on consent. Android advertising privacy settings and Google My Ad Center offer additional choices that vary by device, system version and region. See Google's UMP privacy options documentation.

Purchases.

Google Play handles checkout. The game does not collect payment-card passwords or Google account passwords. To verify and recover orders, necessary order details, signatures and purchase tokens are kept in Android app-private storage. Monthly verification sends the package name, product ID and purchase token over HTTPS to the developer's verification service, which queries Google Play. The service does not write request bodies or tokens to its application logs; the publisher must also review hosting and proxy logging settings.

HTTPS request handling may also involve operational metadata such as connection IP addresses and request times. The fields actually logged, their purposes and retention periods must be confirmed and disclosed before publication.

Local problem records.

Up to 120 local status events help diagnose loading, advertising, billing and screen issues. Fields are filtered; free text, account passwords, purchase tokens and receipt bodies are not included. These diagnostics are not automatically uploaded.

Sharing.

You initiate sharing and select the destination in the system share menu. Browsers without system sharing may attempt to copy the share text and link. The game does not access contacts for sharing and does not send messages automatically.

The destination app or website handles shared content under its own policy.

Retention and deletion.

Reset progress in Settings to clear game records. System app-storage clearing or uninstalling removes app-local data. Google manages its own purchase and subscription records. Removing game data does not cancel a subscription; manage or cancel renewal through Google Play subscriptions.

Manage subscriptions through Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling does not automatically delete advertising or transaction data already processed by Google; use the request channels in Google's Privacy Policy. Any operating system backup or restoration depends on app configuration and device settings; the game's backup configuration must be checked before publication.

Contact messages and privacy requests.

If you email us, we process the email address, message and attachments you provide to answer questions, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, full payment details or purchase tokens. Related messages are retained only as needed to handle requests and meet applicable obligations, then deleted or anonymized.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, request portability and complain to a local data protection authority. Contact changzai.joy@gmail.com. We may request the minimum information needed to verify a request. Device-only records generally need to be deleted on your device; requests concerning data independently processed by Google should also use Google's channels.

Third-party services, international processing and security.

Google and partners providing its services may process information outside your country or region. See Google's Privacy Policy for its practices and applicable transfer safeguards. The hosting location, providers and international processing of the developer's verification service remain to be confirmed. External links and sharing destinations have their own privacy policies.

Order verification uses HTTPS. We will use reasonable safeguards appropriate to our processing, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Audience and children's privacy.

Publisher confirmation required: the intended age range, whether the game is directed to children, distribution regions and corresponding advertising and consent settings have not been supplied. Complete this section for the actual audience and verify store audience declarations and SDK settings before publication. Parents or guardians with questions about children's information can contact the email above.

Changes.

We will update this policy and its effective date when SDKs, data flows or services change. Material changes will be notified, and renewed consent obtained, where required.

Kitty Goes Home 隐私政策（发布审核草稿）

状态：草稿，尚未作为正式隐私政策发布。开发者名称和联系邮箱沿用本仓库现有政策中的 ChangzaiJoy 与 changzai.joy@gmail.com，发布前请核实是否同样适用于本游戏。生效日期应在正式发布时填写。下方列出的实际配置及数据处理事项仍须核实，随后通过 HTTPS 地址发布。

Kitty Goes Home（以下简称“本游戏”）由 ChangzaiJoy 提供。有关本政策或数据处理的问题，请联系 changzai.joy@gmail.com。

1. 本地游戏数据

游戏进度、星级、每日记录、收集内容、语言和音频偏好保存在您的设备上。本游戏不提供独立游戏账号或云端进度同步。重新安装、清除应用数据或更换设备可能导致这些记录丢失。

2. 广告与隐私选择

本游戏使用 Google AdMob 和用户消息平台（User Messaging Platform，UMP）。根据配置、地区及适用的同意选择，这些服务可能处理设备标识符、IP 地址、广告展示和互动、性能及诊断信息，用于广告投放、效果衡量和防范欺诈。IP 地址也可能用于估算大致位置。非个性化广告仍可能为投放、衡量及防欺诈处理数据。

在适用地区，您可以通过游戏内提供的广告隐私选项管理或撤回相关选择。需要同意的处理应在取得必要同意后进行；撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意进行的处理。您也可使用 Android 提供的广告隐私设置和 Google 我的广告中心；可用选项因设备、系统版本和地区而异。

请参阅 Google 隐私政策、AdMob 数据披露指南和 UMP 隐私选项说明。

3. 购买与订单验证

Google Play 负责结账。本游戏不收集支付卡密码或 Google 账号密码。为验证和恢复订单，必要的订单详情、签名和购买令牌保存在 Android 应用私有存储空间中。每月验证会通过 HTTPS 将应用包名、商品 ID 和购买令牌发送至开发者的验证服务，由该服务查询 Google Play。

该服务不会将请求正文或令牌写入应用日志；发布方还须核实托管平台和代理服务器的日志设置。HTTPS 请求处理还可能涉及连接 IP、请求时间等运行元数据，其具体记录字段、用途和保留期限须在发布前确认并披露。

4. 本地问题记录

最多保留 120 条本地状态事件，用于诊断加载、广告、计费和界面问题。记录字段经过过滤，不包含自由文本、账号密码、购买令牌或收据正文。这些诊断记录不会自动上传。

5. 分享

分享由您主动发起，并由您在系统分享菜单中选择目标。不支持系统分享的浏览器可能会尝试将分享文字和链接复制到剪贴板。本游戏不会为分享而访问通讯录，也不会自动发送消息。您选择的目标应用或网站将按其自身政策处理分享内容。

6. 保存与删除

您可以在游戏设置中重置进度以清除游戏记录。通过系统清除应用存储空间或卸载应用可移除应用本地数据。操作系统如提供备份或恢复功能，其行为取决于应用配置及设备设置；发布前须核实本游戏的备份配置。

Google 自行管理其购买和订阅记录。清除游戏数据不会取消订阅，请通过 Google Play 订阅管理管理或取消续订。卸载游戏也不会自动删除 Google 已处理的广告或交易数据，相关请求可通过 Google 隐私政策列明的渠道提交。

7. 联系反馈与隐私请求

如果您通过邮件联系我们，我们会处理您主动提供的邮箱地址、邮件正文和附件，用于回复咨询、排查问题及处理隐私请求。请勿发送密码、完整支付资料或购买令牌。相关邮件仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需的期间内保留，之后删除或匿名化。

根据适用法律，您可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制或反对处理、数据可携带及向当地数据保护机构投诉等权利。请联系 changzai.joy@gmail.com。我们可能要求提供处理请求所需的最少核实信息。仅保存在您设备上的记录通常需要由您在设备上删除；Google 独立处理的数据应同时通过其相应渠道提出请求。

8. 第三方服务、跨境处理与安全

Google 及参与服务提供的合作伙伴可能在您所在国家或地区之外处理信息，其处理规则及适用的跨境保护措施见 Google 隐私政策。开发者验证服务的托管地点、服务提供商及跨境情况仍待核实。第三方链接和分享目标适用其各自的隐私政策。
