Kitty Goes Home is provided by ChangzaiJoy. Contact changzai.joy@gmail.com with questions about this policy or data handling.

Local game data.

Progress, stars, daily records, collections, language and audio preferences are stored on your device. The game has no separate developer-operated game account or cloud progress synchronization. Google Play Games achievement records are handled separately as described below; achievement synchronization does not back up your local game save. Reinstallation, clearing app data or changing devices may remove local records.

Advertising.

The game displays advertising through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Depending on SDK version, configuration and applicable choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect and share IP addresses, device or account identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID), app and ad interactions, and performance and diagnostic information. This information supports advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's disclosure guidance.

IP addresses may indicate approximate location. Non-personalized ads may still process data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required, the game must obtain consent before the relevant processing and provide an in-app entry point to manage or withdraw choices; withdrawal does not affect prior processing based on consent. The publisher must confirm the actual consent platform, including whether Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is integrated, and the privacy options entry point before publication. Android advertising privacy settings and Google My Ad Center offer additional choices that vary by device, system version and region. See Google's UMP privacy options documentation.

Google Play Games achievements.

The game integrates Google Play Games Services to unlock, update and display achievements. When connected to Play Games, achievement identifiers, unlock status and incremental achievement progress, where used, are sent to Google and associated with your Play Games player identity. The game may receive your player identifier and profile information such as your gamer name and avatar to provide these features. Google stores achievement records on its servers so they can be available for the same Play Games profile across supported devices.

The Play Games SDK also collects analytics and diagnostics for service stability and improvement. Data processed by this service is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Profile and game activity visibility depend on your Play Games privacy settings. Manage the profile used for the game and its visibility through your Play Games settings. See Google's Play Games data disclosure and Play Games profile privacy settings.

Purchases.

Google Play handles checkout. The game does not collect payment-card passwords or Google account passwords. To verify and recover orders, necessary order details, signatures and purchase tokens are kept in Android app-private storage. Monthly verification sends the package name, product ID and purchase token over HTTPS to the developer's verification service, which queries Google Play. The service does not write request bodies or tokens to its application logs; the publisher must also review hosting and proxy logging settings.

HTTPS request handling may also involve operational metadata such as connection IP addresses and request times. The fields actually logged, their purposes and retention periods must be confirmed and disclosed before publication.

Local problem records.

Up to 120 local status events help diagnose loading, advertising, billing and screen issues. Fields are filtered; free text, account passwords, purchase tokens and receipt bodies are not included. These diagnostics are not automatically uploaded.

Sharing.

You initiate sharing and select the destination in the system share menu. Browsers without system sharing may attempt to copy the share text and link. The game does not access contacts for sharing and does not send messages automatically.

The destination app or website handles shared content under its own policy.

Retention and deletion.

Reset progress in Settings to clear game records. System app-storage clearing or uninstalling removes app-local data. Google manages its own purchase and subscription records. Removing game data does not cancel a subscription; manage or cancel renewal through Google Play subscriptions.

Resetting local progress or uninstalling the game does not by itself delete achievement records stored by Google Play Games. To remove those records, use your Play Games profile settings to delete this game's Play Games data or your Play Games profile. See Google's Play Games data deletion instructions. This may remove achievements; reconnecting and playing may submit achievement data again. Deleting Play Games data does not delete separately held local data or developer verification records.

Manage subscriptions through Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling does not automatically delete advertising or transaction data already processed by Google; use the request channels in Google's Privacy Policy. Any operating system backup or restoration depends on app configuration and device settings; the game's backup configuration must be checked before publication.

Contact messages and privacy requests.

If you email us, we process the email address, message and attachments you provide to answer questions, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, full payment details or purchase tokens. Related messages are retained only as needed to handle requests and meet applicable obligations, then deleted or anonymized.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, request portability and complain to a local data protection authority. Contact changzai.joy@gmail.com. We may request the minimum information needed to verify a request. Device-only records generally need to be deleted on your device; requests concerning data independently processed by Google should also use Google's channels.

Third-party services, international processing and security.

Google and partners providing its services may process information outside your country or region. See Google's Privacy Policy for its practices and applicable transfer safeguards. The hosting location, providers and international processing of the developer's verification service remain to be confirmed. External links and sharing destinations have their own privacy policies.

Order verification uses HTTPS. We will use reasonable safeguards appropriate to our processing, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Complete this section for the actual audience and verify store audience declarations and SDK settings before publication. Parents or guardians with questions about children's information can contact the email above.

Changes.

We will update this policy and its effective date when SDKs, data flows or services change. Material changes will be notified, and renewed consent obtained, where required.

1. 本地游戏数据

游戏进度、星级、每日记录、收集内容、语言和音频偏好保存在您的设备上。本游戏不提供由开发者运营的独立游戏账号或云端进度同步。Google Play Games 成就记录由下文所述服务单独处理；成就同步不等于本地游戏存档备份。重新安装、清除应用数据或更换设备可能导致本地记录丢失。

2. 广告与隐私选择

本游戏通过 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）展示广告。根据 SDK 版本、配置及适用选择，Google 及其广告合作伙伴可能收集和共享 IP 地址、设备或账号标识符（例如 Android 广告 ID 和应用集 ID）、应用及广告互动、性能及诊断信息，用于广告投放、分析和防范欺诈。IP 地址也可能用于估算大致位置。非个性化广告仍可能为投放、衡量及防欺诈处理数据。Google 说明其 Mobile Ads SDK 收集的数据通过 TLS 加密传输。

在适用要求下，游戏应在相关处理前取得必要同意，并提供游戏内入口供您管理或撤回选择；撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意进行的处理。发布方须在正式发布前确认实际采用的同意平台、是否接入 Google 用户消息平台（User Messaging Platform，UMP），以及隐私选项的实际入口。您也可使用 Android 提供的广告隐私设置和 Google 我的广告中心；可用选项因设备、系统版本和地区而异。

请参阅 Google 隐私政策、AdMob 数据披露指南和 UMP 隐私选项说明。

3. Google Play Games 成就系统

本游戏接入 Google Play Games Services，用于解锁、更新和展示成就。连接 Play Games 后，成就标识、解锁状态以及使用增量成就时的完成进度会发送给 Google，并与您的 Play Games 玩家身份关联。为提供这些功能，游戏可能获取玩家标识以及玩家昵称、头像等资料。Google 在其服务器上保存成就记录，使同一 Play Games 玩家资料可在支持的设备上使用这些记录。

Play Games SDK 还会收集用于服务稳定性和改进的分析及诊断信息，该服务处理的数据通过 HTTPS 加密传输。玩家资料和游戏活动的可见范围取决于您的 Play Games 隐私设置。您可以在 Play Games 设置中管理本游戏使用的玩家资料及其可见范围。请参阅 Play Games 数据披露说明和 Play Games 玩家资料隐私设置。

4. 购买与订单验证

Google Play 负责结账。本游戏不收集支付卡密码或 Google 账号密码。为验证和恢复订单，必要的订单详情、签名和购买令牌保存在 Android 应用私有存储空间中。每月验证会通过 HTTPS 将应用包名、商品 ID 和购买令牌发送至开发者的验证服务，由该服务查询 Google Play。

该服务不会将请求正文或令牌写入应用日志；发布方还须核实托管平台和代理服务器的日志设置。HTTPS 请求处理还可能涉及连接 IP、请求时间等运行元数据，其具体记录字段、用途和保留期限须在发布前确认并披露。

5. 本地问题记录

最多保留 120 条本地状态事件，用于诊断加载、广告、计费和界面问题。记录字段经过过滤，不包含自由文本、账号密码、购买令牌或收据正文。这些诊断记录不会自动上传。

6. 分享

分享由您主动发起，并由您在系统分享菜单中选择目标。不支持系统分享的浏览器可能会尝试将分享文字和链接复制到剪贴板。本游戏不会为分享而访问通讯录，也不会自动发送消息。您选择的目标应用或网站将按其自身政策处理分享内容。

7. 保存与删除

您可以在游戏设置中重置进度以清除游戏记录。通过系统清除应用存储空间或卸载应用可移除应用本地数据。操作系统如提供备份或恢复功能，其行为取决于应用配置及设备设置；发布前须核实本游戏的备份配置。

重置本地进度或卸载游戏本身不会删除 Google Play Games 保存的成就记录。如需删除，请在 Play Games 玩家资料设置中删除本游戏的 Play Games 数据或您的 Play Games 玩家资料，操作方式见 Google 的 Play Games 数据删除说明。这可能移除成就；重新连接并继续游玩后，游戏可能再次提交成就数据。删除 Play Games 数据不会删除另外保存的本地数据或开发者订单验证记录。

Google 自行管理其购买和订阅记录。清除游戏数据不会取消订阅，请通过 Google Play 订阅管理管理或取消续订。卸载游戏也不会自动删除 Google 已处理的广告或交易数据，相关请求可通过 Google 隐私政策列明的渠道提交。
