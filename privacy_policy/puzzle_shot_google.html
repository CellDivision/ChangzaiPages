1. Game features and local data

The Game is provided free of charge. Game data and settings retained on your device are used to provide and improve the game experience. We do not require you to submit personally identifying information directly to us to use the Game. This does not mean that third-party services process no information; their processing is described below.

The Game uses Google Play services and Google AdMob. These services may process device and usage information under their own privacy policies. Loading and displaying ads requires an internet connection.

2. AdMob advertising and information it processes

The Game uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertising. AdMob includes advertising measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

Depending on the SDK version, device settings, applicable region, and your privacy choices, AdMob collects and shares the following types of information:

  • IP address: which may be used to estimate a device’s general location.
  • App and ad interactions: such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics: such as app and SDK performance, startup time, hangs, and energy usage.
  • Device and account identifiers: such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device.

This information supports ad delivery, ad measurement, advertising service analytics, and fraud and abuse prevention. Ad personalization depends on applicable rules, advertising settings, and your choices. Non-personalized ads may also process some data for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google and partners involved in ad delivery may store, access, and process information through SDKs, device identifiers, or similar technologies. See Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and AdMob privacy information.

3. Use and disclosure

Device-local game data supports game features and settings. Google Play services process information to provide their services, and AdMob and its advertising partners process advertising data for the purposes described above. Third-party diagnostic information may also be used to identify errors and improve reliability. We may disclose information where required by applicable law.

If you contact us by email, we process the email address, message, and attachments you voluntarily provide only to handle inquiries, bug reports, privacy requests, and necessary related records. Please avoid sending unrelated sensitive information. We do not disclose your contact information to third parties except for service processing described in this policy or when legally required.

4. Retention, deletion, and security

Game data stored locally normally remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game. You can clear data from the app’s storage page in Android settings; this deletes local progress and settings. Any applicable operating system backup or restoration is managed by your device and system settings. We do not operate that backup service.

Contact emails are retained only as needed to resolve requests, provide necessary follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations, and are then deleted or anonymized. You may request deletion using the email below. If information must be retained, we will explain why.

Google retains and deletes advertising data under its policies and applicable requirements. Clearing local data or uninstalling the Game does not automatically delete advertising data already processed by Google. You may submit requests using Google’s privacy tools or the methods listed in its policies.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to our processing. According to Google’s SDK data disclosure, user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

You can use available Android advertising privacy settings to manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID; options vary by system version. You can also manage applicable Google ad preferences in Google My Ad Center. These actions may not stop all advertising or all advertising data processing.

Where consent is required, relevant advertising data processing should take place only after the necessary consent is obtained. Where the Game provides an advertising consent message or privacy options entry point under applicable requirements, you can use it to manage or withdraw relevant choices. Withdrawal does not affect processing based on consent before withdrawal.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, obtain data portability, and complain to a local data protection authority. Contact us using the email below. For information independently processed by Google, you can also exercise rights through Google’s privacy tools.

6. Intended age and minors’ privacy

The Game is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personally identifying information directly from minors under 18. Advertising SDK processing is described in Section 2.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor under 18 has provided personal information to us while using the Game, please contact us at the email below. We will investigate and take applicable deletion or other protective measures for relevant information we hold. For information independently processed by Google, we will explain the relevant privacy request channels.

Google and its partners may process advertising data on servers outside your country or region. Please consult Google’s Privacy Policy for applicable international transfer safeguards. 8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when game features, advertising services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. For material changes, we will provide notice and obtain renewed consent where required.
